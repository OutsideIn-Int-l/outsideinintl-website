A Living Heritage

Across the South-West, Yoruba traditions remain strong: festivals are actively celebrated, royal institutions remain respected, and arts and crafts thrive. Expect music and drumming, traditional attire, oral storytelling, and vibrant festivals and ceremonies.

A Taste of the South-West

Food is central to hospitality and cultural identity. Popular dishes include Amala with Ewedu and Gbegiri, Iyan (pounded yam), Eba, Ikokore, Ofada rice with Ayamase sauce, Efo riro, Akara and Moimoi, Boli, Dodo Ikire, and suya and grilled meats.
