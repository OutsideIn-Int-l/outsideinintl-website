About Us

Who We Are

Outside In International Limited is a professional immigration, relocation and travel experience management company based in Nigeria — delivering fast, compliant and seamless solutions across the full immigration lifecycle.

Executive Summary

More than a visa-processing company

We provide end-to-end immigration and relocation solutions that make cross-border entry into Nigeria simple, compliant and stress-free. Outside In acts as a full-service immigration, compliance, expatriate mobility, investor advisory and premium relocation partner.

We are also passionate about showcasing the very best of Nigeria. Beyond immigration and relocation, we provide secure, well-curated cultural, leisure and business-friendly experiences that help foreign visitors discover the true Nigeria in a safe, comfortable and authentic way.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted immigration, relocation and travel experience partner. Fast. Seamless. Compliant.

Our Mission

To redefine inbound immigration, relocation and travel experiences in Africa through exceptional service, rapid turnaround and unmatched compliance expertise — making cross-border entry and settlement simple for individuals and businesses.
