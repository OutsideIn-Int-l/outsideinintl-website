Our Service Structure

Three Pillars of Support

End-to-end support for individuals and businesses entering Nigeria — from visas and permits to investment structuring, settlement and curated travel experiences.

A

Immigration & Compliance Services

Visas, permits, quota management, advisory and audits — fully aligned with Nigeria's digital Expatriate Administration System (EAS).

Short Visit Visas

Business, tourist and transit visas under Nigeria's e-Visa regime — documentation, submission, embassy liaison and follow-up.

Value: Smooth, timely entry with zero delays or rejection risk.

Work & Residency Permits

Processing and renewal of e-CERPAC, eTWP and STR permits for long-stay expatriates — with the new e-CERPAC secured before entry into Nigeria.

Value: Full legal compliance for foreign employees from day one.

Expatriate Quota Management

Management of quota applications, utilisation, renewals and reporting through the government's online expatriate quota platform.

Value: Legal foreign hiring with regulatory risk eliminated.

Immigration Advisory & Retainerships

Ongoing compliance support for individuals and corporate organisations.

Value: Legal compliance, audit readiness and expert guidance on demand.

Regulatory Compliance Audits

Assessment of immigration documentation, permits and compliance gaps across your workforce.

Value: Prevents penalties and ensures audit readiness.

Visa Deactivation on Exit

Management of immigration exit documentation and closure processes when expatriates depart.

Value: Clean compliance records after departure — no barriers at re-entry.
B

Business & Investor Services

Advisory and government liaison for investors and founders; logistics support for international projects and conferences.

Investor & Founder Immigration Advisory

Immigration advisory and business-entry structuring for investors and foreign founders entering Nigeria, including business permit support.

Value: Compliant market entry and penetration.

Government Relations Liaison

Representation and engagement with immigration authorities and government agencies on your behalf.

Value: Faster approvals and resolution of complex cases.

International Conferencing Logistics

Immigration and logistics support for international projects, delegations and conferences hosted in Nigeria.

Value: Frictionless entry for teams and delegates, however large.
C

Relocation & Lifestyle Services

Settlement support, concierge care and secured domestic travel experiences.

Relocation & Expatriate Integration

Support with housing, schools, healthcare, banking and relocation logistics for arriving expatriates and their families.

Value: Fast settlement and a smooth transition.

Premium Concierge Services

VIP immigration, travel and lifestyle management for employees, owners, investors and high-net-worth individuals.

Value: Seamless, discreet, high-end relocation experiences.

Secured Tourist Visits

Organised, secure explorative travel across Nigeria with transportation and accommodation support — specially curated experiences for visitors.

Value: Safe, memorable exploration that reshapes perceptions of Nigeria.
