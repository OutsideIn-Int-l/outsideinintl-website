Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which visas can you help foreigners obtain for Nigeria?

We handle the full range under Nigeria's e-Visa regime — business, tourist and transit visas — plus long-stay routes: eTWP, STR and the new e-CERPAC residence permit, which is now processed before you enter Nigeria.

Do you support companies with expatriate quota and compliance?

Yes. We manage expatriate quota applications, utilisation, renewals and reporting through the government's digital Expatriate Administration System, plus compliance audits and ongoing advisory retainerships.

How long does the immigration process take?

It varies by route: short visit visas are typically fast, while work and residency permits depend on agency timelines. We give you a realistic timeline at consultation and manage every follow-up to keep your case moving.

Do you offer relocation and settlement support as well?

Yes — housing, schools, healthcare, banking and relocation logistics, premium concierge services, and secure curated travel experiences across Nigeria.

How do I get started?

Book a consultation — by form, phone or WhatsApp. We assess your situation, recommend the right route, and handle everything from there.
