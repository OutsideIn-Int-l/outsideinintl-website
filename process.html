Our Approach

End-to-End Immigration Management

From first consultation to final approval — and beyond — our team manages every step of the immigration journey on your behalf.

Five Steps, One Partner

Every case follows a disciplined, transparent process designed for speed, compliance and zero friction.

  1. Consultation & Assessment

    We understand your situation — individual or corporate — and assess the right visa, permit or entry route under current Nigerian regulations.

    Typically 1 to 3 days

    What we handle

    • Assess your eligibility and options
    • Recommend the right visa or permit route
    • Outline documents, costs and timelines

    What you provide

    • Your purpose and intended dates
    • Passport and basic background details
    • Any company or sponsor information

  2. Documentation Preparation

    We compile, review and perfect every required document before anything is submitted, eliminating rejection risk.

    Typically 3 to 7 days

    What we handle

    • Prepare and complete application forms
    • Draft invitation and support letters
    • Review every document for accuracy

    What you provide

    • Requested supporting documents
    • Photographs and signatures
    • Prompt confirmation of details

  3. Submission & Government Liaison

    We lodge your application through the correct digital channels — the e-Visa portal, EAS and NIS platforms — then engage directly with the authorities to keep your case moving.

    Timeline varies by agency

    What we handle

    • Submit via the e-Visa portal, EAS and NIS platforms
    • Liaise and follow up with immigration authorities
    • Escalate and resolve delays where needed

    What you provide

    • Any additional documents requested
    • Availability for biometrics if required
    • Payment of official fees

  4. Approval Management

    We track, follow up and secure approvals — keeping you informed at every stage.

    On decision

    What we handle

    • Track the case and secure the approval
    • Confirm the outcome and hand over documents

    What you provide

    • Acknowledgement of handover

  5. Compliance, Renewal & Exit

    After approval, we monitor your ongoing obligations, manage renewals ahead of deadlines and — when the time comes — handle exit documentation and visa deactivation for a clean departure record.

    Ongoing · ahead of expiry · on departure

    What we handle

    • Monitor reporting and keep you audit-ready
    • Manage permit renewals before deadlines
    • Handle exit documentation and visa deactivation

    What you provide

    • Notice of any change in role or plans
    • Instructions for renewals or departure dates
Application→ Approval→ Compliance→ Renewal→ Exit

Indicative timings are for guidance only and vary by visa type, agency and how quickly documents are supplied. We confirm exact timelines during your consultation.
